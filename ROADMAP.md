# Roadmap

These are proposed milestones, not shipped capabilities or delivery commitments.

## 0.1 — Invention intake preview (included)

- Self-contained skill and output conventions.
- Source-linked fictional example.
- Adversarial cases and evaluation rubric.
- Package integrity checks.

## 0.2 — Filing-review preview (included)

- Configurable company business criteria with scales, weights, evidence, and approval state.
- Unknown-preserving scores and separate legal decision status.
- Supplied strategy mappings, tag-based portfolio candidates, and family/status counts.
- Deterministic incremental budget scenarios with baseline, reserve, period handling, and quarterly costs.
- Fifteen calculator tests and a reproducible fictional demonstration.

This version performs exact tag matching, not semantic retrieval or claim-level coverage analysis.

## 0.3 — Portfolio-strategy preview (this release)

- Source-linked technology buckets, organizational hierarchy, people associations, products, and roadmap targets.
- Structured application/jurisdiction records with family labels, public identifier fields, status dates, claim locators, and mapping provenance.
- JSON/CSV record import and inventory summaries with unresolved metadata.
- Feature/jurisdiction triage, proposed mappings, open disclosure pipeline, and configurable review freshness.
- Recorded counsel assessments kept separate from software inventory signals.
- Compatible strategy/portfolio handoff to filing review, with 20 additional tests.

The analyzer does not perform semantic retrieval, legal status lookup, or claim analysis. Recorded counsel assessments are supplied data, not software-generated or authenticated legal conclusions.

## Next — Validate with practitioners

- Run recorded evaluations on named models.
- Ask patent counsel to review synthetic outputs.
- Measure unsupported assertions, missed contradictions, and editing effort.
- Improve source extraction and diagram review based on observed failures.

## Later — Deeper portfolio review

- Evaluated document/claim retrieval and claim-informed comparison, with counsel-reviewed outputs.
- More robust record deduplication, versioned assessment history, and adapters for actual portfolio exports.
- Practitioner-tested coverage reviews and reporting exports.

## Separately scoped future skills

- Office-action issue mapping and response preparation.
- Claim-support review.
- Prosecution-history summaries.

External search, live interviewing, transcription, document exports, docketing, and professional-system integrations need their own evaluated implementations. They are not provided by this preview.
